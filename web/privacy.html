JPH Housekeeping – Privacy notice

JPH Housekeeping is an internal tool used at the Jorf Phosphate Hub to record housekeeping observations and send daily and weekly reports. It is not a public service.

What is collected

  • Supervisor accounts: username and full name, created by the administrator.
  • Observations: photo, GPS position, time, area, category, severity and comments entered by supervisors.
  • Report recipients: name and e-mail address, entered by the administrator.

How it is used

Data is used only to build housekeeping reports for the site and to follow up open items. It is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

Google Drive

The app uses the Google Drive permission drive.file on the project’s own Google account to archive observation photos and report PDFs. It can only see the files it creates; it cannot read any other file in that Drive.

Storage and retention

Records are stored in a Supabase database. Temporary photo copies are deleted automatically after 30 days; the archive copy stays in Google Drive until the administrator removes it.
